FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Fraylon Industry Readiness Workshop.

1. What is the Fraylon Industry Readiness Workshop?

The Fraylon Industry Readiness Workshop is a practical training program designed to help students gain real-world industry skills through hands-on learning, live sessions, and project-based training.

2. Who can participate in the workshop?

College students, freshers, graduates, and learners interested in improving their technical and professional skills can participate.

3. Is the workshop conducted online or offline?

The workshop is conducted remotely, making it accessible for students from different locations.

4. What skills will participants learn?

Participants will gain industry-oriented technical skills, teamwork experience, communication skills, and practical project execution knowledge.

5. Will participants work on real-time projects?

Yes, the program includes practical assignments and real-world project-based learning activities.

6. Are certificates provided after completion?

Yes, participants will receive a certificate upon successful completion of the workshop program.

7. Is there any internship or full-time opportunity?

Yes, based on performance during the workshop and hackathon activities, selected participants may receive internship or full-time role opportunities with Fraylon Technologies.

8. Do participants need prior experience?

No prior industry experience is required. Beginners and learners at different skill levels can join.

9. How long is the workshop program?

The workshop and training sessions will be conducted from 5th June 2026 to 20th June 2026.

10. What makes this workshop different?

The workshop focuses on practical implementation, industry workflows, mentorship, and collaborative learning instead of only theoretical teaching.

11. Will mentorship be provided during the program?

Yes, participants will receive guidance and mentorship throughout the training process.

12. How can colleges collaborate with Fraylon Technologies?

Colleges can collaborate for workshops, hackathons, technical sessions, and student development initiatives by contacting Fraylon Technologies.

13. Is the workshop suitable for non-technical students?

Yes, both technical and non-technical students can participate depending on the workshop category.

14. How can students register for the workshop?

Students can register through the official registration form or by contacting the Fraylon team directly.
